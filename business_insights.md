Tembo Hotel & Suites — Business Analysis & Insights
Project Overview
This analysis evaluates booking, revenue, guest, staff, occupancy, and cancellation data for Tembo Hotel & Suites. The objective is to identify patterns in hotel performance and provide data-driven insights that can support operational and commercial decision-making.
The analysis was performed using PostgreSQL on the cleaned booking dataset. Results were structured around the six business questions provided in the project brief.
1. Revenue Analysis
Business Question
•	What is the total revenue by month?
•	Which room types generate the most revenue?
•	Which payment methods generate the most revenue?
Key Findings
Monthly Revenue
Revenue varied considerably throughout the analysis period. Monthly revenue increased substantially from late 2023 into early 2024.
•	Highest monthly revenue: March 2024 — KES 803,000
•	Lowest monthly revenue: October 2023 — KES 119,500
•	December 2023 recorded a substantial increase compared with November 2023.
•	Revenue also showed notable declines in May and August 2024, followed by recoveries in subsequent months.
Revenue by Room Type
Room Type	Total Revenue (KES)
Suite	2,731,100
Deluxe	2,406,700
Standard	1,948,800
Penthouse	1,792,600
Suites generated the highest recorded revenue at KES 2,731,100. This should be interpreted alongside booking volume because total revenue is influenced by both room price and the number and duration of bookings.
Revenue by Payment Method
Payment Method	Total Revenue (KES)
Bank Transfer	2,466,500
Cash	2,309,500
M-Pesa	2,108,700
Card	1,994,500
Bank Transfer accounted for the highest recorded revenue among the payment methods, while Card recorded the lowest.

5. Revenue Trends
Business Question
•	What is the month-over-month revenue growth?
•	Which months are busiest and quietest?
Key Findings
The month-over-month analysis showed substantial fluctuations in revenue.
•	Largest monthly increase: December 2023 — 104.59%
•	Second-largest increase: January 2024 — 65.93%
•	Largest monthly decline: May 2024 — -36.93%
•	September 2024 increased by 56.13%
•	June 2024 increased by 53.42%
March 2024 generated the highest monthly revenue, but its month-over-month growth was 10.68%, demonstrating that the highest-revenue month is not necessarily the month with the highest growth rate.
Busiest and Quietest Months

•	June 2024 — 24 bookings
•	October 2024 — 24 bookings
Quietest months:
•	June 2023 — 4 bookings
•	August 2023 — 4 bookings
•	October 2023 — 4 bookings
Booking volume was substantially higher throughout 2024 than during the earlier months represented in the dataset.

Overall Business Insights
The analysis identified several notable patterns:
1.	Standard rooms generated the highest booking volume, with 114 bookings.
2.	Suites generated the highest total recorded revenue, at KES 2,731,100.
3.	Suites also had the longest average stay, at 3.22 nights.
4.	Nairobi was the dominant guest city, with 129 bookings.
5.	March 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue, at KES 803,000.
6.	June and October 2024 were the busiest months, with 24 bookings each.
7.	Penthouse had the highest cancellation rate, at 20.00%.
8.	Cancelled and no-show bookings represented KES 1,175,300 in recorded booking value.
9.	Revenue showed significant month-to-month volatility, with particularly large increases in December 2023 and January 2024 and a notable decline in May 2024.
10.	Guest ratings varied by room type, with Standard and Penthouse averaging 3.13 and Suite averaging 2.88.
Analytical Considerations
The analysis is based on the cleaned booking dataset and describes observed patterns rather than establishing causation.
Two records, BK9002 and BK9003, contain invalid date sequences where the check-out date occurs before the check-in date. These records should be excluded from analyses that depend on valid stay duration or date intervals.
In addition, missing values were retained where the correct value could not be reliably determined from the available data. For example, BK9003 retains a NULL nights_stayed and total_amount.
For cancellation analysis, the reported KES 1,175,300 represents recorded booking values associated with cancelled and no-show reservations. The dataset does not provide sufficient information to determine the actual amount financially lost by the hotel.
