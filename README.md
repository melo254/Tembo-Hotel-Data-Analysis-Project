# Tembo Hotel & Suites - Business Problem

**Tembo Hotel & Suites** is a mid-range business hotel in Nairobi that has been operating since 2023. The hotel has historically maintained its booking records in spreadsheets, resulting in a dataset containing inconsistencies, missing values, duplicate records, and other data-quality issues.

The Hotel Director needs a clear understanding of the hotel's overall performance and has tasked the data team with turning the raw booking data into reliable business insights.

### Business Questions

The analysis will focus on:

* **Revenue:** Which room types and payment methods generate the most revenue, and how does revenue change by month?
* **Occupancy:** Which room types are booked most frequently, and how long do guests typically stay?
* **Guests:** Where do the hotel's guests come from, and how do they rate different room types?
* **Staff:** Which staff members handle the most bookings, and which departments are associated with the highest recorded revenue?
* **Trends:** Which months are busiest and quietest, and what is the month-over-month revenue growth?
* **Cancellations:** Which room types have the highest cancellation rates, and what booking value is associated with cancellations and no-shows?

### Project Objective

The objective is to **clean and analyse the hotel's booking data using PostgreSQL and SQL, then present the findings through a professional Power BI dashboard** that gives management a clear view of hotel performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL | SQL | Power BI
